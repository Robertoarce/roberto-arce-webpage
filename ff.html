01

POINTER · MOVE

The surface leans in.

Wherever the pointer travels, a soft dome of light rises out of the grid and glides after it — faster movement feeds it more energy. The camera drifts on a parallax axis while the drift of bodies — solid crystals, hollow wireframes, mirror plates strung together by constellation lines — keeps a polite distance from your hand.

Try quick figure-eights, then hold still and watch the dome breathe back down.

02

MOUSE BUTTON · CLICK

Every click lands like a stone.

A click is raycast onto the water and dropped in as an expanding ring — up to four waves overlap and interfere at once. A flash of light kicks the shards outward, the wireframes flare, and the constellation briefly stretches its reach before settling back.

Click rapidly in one spot to stack the ripples.

03

WHEEL · SCROLL

Scroll is depth, not distance.

Scrolling doesn't just move the page: it pushes the camera low across the water, thickens the swell, speeds the turning of every shard, and re-tunes the entire palette — teal gives way to amber, the fog warms, and even the interface accents shift with you.
